## History

The **Wrenhaven Light** ([/ˈrɛnheɪvən/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English) [*REN-hay-vən*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key); [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Phare de Wrenhaven*) is a [cast-iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cast_iron) [lighthouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lighthouse) on the [headland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headland) above [the old harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harbor), on the west coast.[1] Its height is `h=41` metres.

- Built for the [Harbour Board's 1871 plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrenhaven_Harbour_Board_(1871))
- Repainted every four years
