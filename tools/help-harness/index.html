The lighthouse keeper

Live, anonymous Planning notes

Every evening the keeper climbed the hundred and twelve steps and counted them aloud, though she had known the number for thirty years. The counting was not for the steps.

Below, the sea worked at the rocks the way it always had, patiently, without malice, and the light swung out over it like a question nobody needed to answer.
