# Notes from the Harbour

# Morning

The boats came in at dawn. **Forty** of them, *maybe more*, and one with a ~~broken~~ mast. “Don’t count them,” said the harbourmaster — he’d tried.

- Mackerel
  - Small ones, thrown back
- Herring

Then the tide tables:

1. High water at 06:12
2. Low water at 12:30

> The sea does not care about your schedule.

Read more at [the tide office](https://www.example.org/tides?port=dover&day=1).[1]

## Evening

***All* hands** ashore by nine.
